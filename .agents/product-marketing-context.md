# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-10*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** OwnCanvas is a local-first open creative canvas for operators who already pay for AI tools.
**What it does:** OwnCanvas gives creative operators one canvas for text, image, video, and voice generation blocks. It is designed around bringing your own provider accounts, subscriptions, keys, and models instead of buying another closed AI canvas subscription.
**Product category:** AI creative canvas, local-first AI workspace, campaign creative production tool.
**Product type:** Open-source local-first desktop/web app.
**Business model:** Open-source first; monetization can later come from hosted sync, team collaboration, templates, or paid distribution.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Solo founders, creators, small agencies, in-house marketing teams, and content teams using multiple AI generation providers.
**Decision-makers:** Founder, marketer, creative lead, agency operator, content lead, designer-marketer.
**Primary use case:** Build campaign creative outputs without bouncing between separate AI subscriptions and tools.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Create and compare campaign copy, images, videos, and voice assets in one place.
- Reuse the AI provider accounts and subscriptions the operator already pays for.
- Turn repeated creative production patterns into reusable workflows later.
**Use cases:**
- Launch campaign creative pack
- Product image and short-form video ideation
- Ad variant production
- Social content production
- Agency client concept exploration

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Creative operators use several AI generation tools but lose context, money, and speed when every provider has its own closed workspace.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Closed canvas tools add another subscription instead of using the accounts users already have.
- Single-provider tools make it hard to compare models and outputs.
- Workflow automation tools expose too much graph complexity too early.
**What it costs them:** Extra subscriptions, duplicated prompts, scattered assets, slower campaign production, and weaker reuse.
**Emotional tension:** "I already pay for the models. Why am I paying again for the canvas?"

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Freepik AI Suite, Higgsfield Canvas, Macro, Pixloop, Canva AI tools — fall short when users want local-first control and bring-your-own-provider economics.
**Secondary:** n8n, ComfyUI, Zapier-style automation — fall short because they are workflow-first, not campaign-output-first.
**Indirect:** Manual tool hopping between ChatGPT, image tools, video tools, and voice tools — falls short because the operator loses context and comparison.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Local-first creative canvas
- Bring-your-own provider accounts, keys, subscriptions, and models
- Campaign-first rather than workflow-first
- Open-source positioning
**How we do it differently:** OwnCanvas starts with a canvas for campaign outputs, then lets workflows emerge from repeated generation blocks.
**Why that's better:** Operators get value immediately without learning a graph builder.
**Why customers choose us:** They want a reusable workspace without adding another closed AI subscription.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| I can already use ChatGPT and separate tools. | OwnCanvas keeps the campaign context, assets, and provider choices together. |
| Workflow tools already exist. | OwnCanvas is campaign-output-first; workflow extraction comes later. |
| Local setup sounds harder. | The target user already manages AI tools and values control over another subscription. |

**Anti-persona:** Users who want a fully hosted, single-provider, no-setup consumer design app.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Tool switching, subscription fatigue, scattered prompts and outputs.
**Pull:** One local canvas that uses existing AI accounts.
**Habit:** Users already know individual provider tools and may not want setup work.
**Anxiety:** Local provider setup, API keys, and whether output quality matches closed tools.
